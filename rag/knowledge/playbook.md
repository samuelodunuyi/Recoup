# Recovery playbook — general principles

General (processor- and code-agnostic) guidance retrieved alongside the specific
decline-code knowledge. These sections carry no decline code.

## Tone and trust
Lead with warmth, never blame. A failed payment is a friction, not a moral failing.
Use the customer's name, keep messages short (WhatsApp, not email), and sound like
a helpful human. Never threaten suspension in the first contact; mention it only as
a gentle, factual consequence late in a sequence if needed.

## Timing around paydays
Recovery rates rise sharply when retries are timed to when money is available. Ask
or infer payday and propose that date. "Should I try again on Friday when you're
paid?" converts far better than repeated immediate retries. Always confirm the day
back to the customer so the commitment is explicit.

## Honouring commitments
If a customer promised to pay at a time, remember it and reference it on the next
turn ("you mentioned Friday — want me to send the link now?"). Never re-ask for
information already given, and never repeat the same message twice.

## Language and localisation
Match the customer's language and register. For Nigerian customers comfortable in
Pidgin, replying in warm Pidgin builds rapport ("No wahala, the payment no go
through. I fit send you fresh link make you try again?"). Keep it natural, not
caricatured.

## When to escalate
Escalate to a human for fraud or dispute claims you cannot resolve, abusive
messages, legal threats, or repeated confusion after two clear explanations. A
clean handoff preserves trust better than an agent stuck in a loop.
